What could be automated?

The best opportunities are usually the small, repetitive jobs that happen every day — particularly where people are moving information between systems.

Moving information between systems

Reduce copying between email, spreadsheets, CRMs and industry-specific software.

Document processing

Extract, classify and route useful information from forms, PDFs, attachments and incoming emails.

Repetitive communication

Streamline routine notifications, follow-ups, acknowledgements and status updates.

Workflow coordination

Connect the steps around approvals, maintenance, inspections and other recurring processes.

Automate the gaps, not your whole business.

The goal is usually not to replace the software you already rely on. It is to remove the manual work happening between those systems.
